Hariprabodham Parivaar (HP Parivaar) — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Operator: NC Pictures Ltd, Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Contact: admin@ncpictures.ca

Hariprabodham Parivaar ("HP Parivaar", "the app", "we", "us") is a private mobile app for local satsang mandals. It is operated by NC Pictures Ltd. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. By creating an account you confirm that you have read it.

1. Who the app is for

HP Parivaar is for people who have been invited into a local mandal. After you sign up and confirm your email, an App Admin Karya-Karta of your mandal must approve you before you can see anything inside that mandal.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: email address and password (or, if you use Google or Apple sign-in, the name and email those services share with us). Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us or to other members.
  • Profile details: first and last name, your Bhai (Yuvak) or Ben (Yuvati) section, your mandal, the city, province and country linked to that mandal, the member categories you choose, and an optional birthday (day and month, and year if you enter it).
  • Optional profile details: profile photo or avatar colour, job title, company, business description, industry, website, a short bio, and a phone number. Your phone number is hidden from other members unless you turn on "Show phone in the directory".
  • Content you add: birthday messages (gunanuvaad), poll votes and answers, event registrations and party size, seva sign-ups and notes, pickup address and ride requests (only if you ask for a ride), job posts and their contact details, and reports you file about content.
  • Content App Admin Karya-Karta add: events, calendar entries, announcements, posts, documents, polls, emergency seva requests and sabha photo albums for their mandal.
  • Mandal administration: requests to join or change mandal, the join code you enter, and the referral note you may add. For mandal administration we also keep the email addresses of people appointed as local administrators.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, we store a device push token so we can send you reminders. You can turn notifications off in the app or in your device settings, and you can choose which categories you receive.
  • In-app notices: a list of notifications sent to you (for example birthday and event reminders).
  • Technical data our service providers process to run the service, such as IP address and basic request logs held by our hosting and authentication providers.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking software in the app, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not request your precise location or access your contacts.

Device permissions. The app asks for access to your camera and photo library only when you choose to add a profile picture or (for App Admin Karya-Karta) sabha pictures, and to save event photos you choose to download. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and confirm your email.
  • To place you in the correct mandal and the correct Bhai/Ben section, and to let App Admin Karya-Karta approve members.
  • To run the features you use: events and calendar, registrations, seva, polls, birthday wishes, photo albums, jobs and the member directory.
  • To send notifications you have not turned off, and account emails such as the welcome and activation email.
  • To keep the community safe, handle reports and blocks, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to your requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions that have legal effect on you.

4. Who can see your information

  • Separation by mandal and section. Each mandal is separate. Members and App Admin Karya-Karta see only the mandal they belong to. Content and jobs are further separated between Bhai (Yuvak) and Ben (Yuvati) sections, so each section sees only its own. These rules are enforced in our database, not only in the app screens.
  • Pending members cannot see mandal content until approved.
  • App Admin Karya-Karta of your mandal can see your profile and sign-up details, approve or remove members, and remove content that breaks the rules.
  • Platform administrators at NC Pictures can access the data as needed to operate, secure and support the service.
  • Other members can see the profile information and content you choose to share inside your mandal (and section where applicable). Your phone number and pickup address are not shown to ordinary members.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage and server functions, and sending account emails.
  • Expo (Expo Push Notification service) — delivers push notifications to your device. It receives your push token and the text of the notification.
  • Apple and Google — if you choose "Sign in with Apple" or "Sign in with Google", and for app distribution and push delivery on their platforms.

Our providers may store and process information on servers in Canada, the United States or other countries, so your information may be subject to the laws of those places. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in, profile, registrations, votes, birthday messages, job posts, reports, blocks, notifications and push token, remove your uploaded photos and profile picture from storage, and remove related records such as your pickup address and join requests. Content a mandal depends on (such as events or announcements you created) may be reassigned to another administrator of that mandal rather than deleted. Limited administrative audit records (for example a record that a local administrator was appointed or a section was corrected) and routine provider backups and logs may remain for a short period for security and legal reasons.

7. Deleting your account and your rights

  • In the app: open the menu or Profile and choose Delete my account. Deletion is permanent.
  • By email: write to admin@ncpictures.ca from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We will verify it is you and complete the request within 30 days.
  • Access and correction: you can view and edit most of your information under Profile. You may also ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent, by emailing admin@ncpictures.ca. The app does not currently offer an automatic data export.
  • Under Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act, you may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta if you have a concern we have not resolved.

8. Children and youth

HP Parivaar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you should use the app only with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian, who may contact us at any time to review or delete the account. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email admin@ncpictures.ca and we will delete it. Photos added to a mandal are visible only inside that mandal (and section, where applicable); a parent or guardian can ask us to remove a photo of their child.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards: encrypted connections, access rules enforced in the database, and restricted administrator access. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you suspect unauthorized access.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make a material change we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
